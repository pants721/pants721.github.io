3D N-body simulator built in C++20. Implements Barnes-Hut octree and direct summation force solvers with OpenMP and Intel TBB parallelization, symplectic leapfrog integration, and real-time OpenGL rendering. Runs everything on the CPU (for now).

Super fun project. I wrote LUNA in order to study for my University Physics I final exam and enrich my understanding of basic orbital mechanics and stuff. On the way I learned a lot about optimization, multithreading, OpenGL, and modern C++. Certainly a lot of room for improvement, but I'm proud of the little amount I was able to optimize it on my own.
